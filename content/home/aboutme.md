I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Leibniz Institute for Astrophysics Potsdam (AIP), Germany, in the [Cosmology and High-Energy Astrophysics section](https://www.aip.de/en/research/cosmology/). I am also an external collaborator of the [DFG Research Unit FOR5195](https://www.for5195.uni-wuerzburg.de).

I did my PhD at Inter-University Center for Astronomy & Astrophysics (IUCAA), Pune. I finished my Bachelor-Master's from Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Mohali. I spent most of my life in the beautiful city of Chandigarh, also known as the City Beautiful. I enjoy exploring nature, hiking, and playing badminton.
